Execution will use the indexed TESTCASE-025 artifact and record timestamped pass, fail, or blocked evidence for each PUB-TC case. Start with the safe tranche: credential-disabled public metadata and HEAD checks, independent and recursive clean clones, public pages and list endpoints, zero-state tags and releases, anonymous write denial by dry run, secret/history scans, ignore-pattern checks in disposable clones, invalid URLs, network-failure handling, concurrent clones, pagination/rate limits, and former-name redirects. No account-scoped or repository-changing action is authorized in that tranche. The controlled tranche remains gated: PUB-TC-006 and 007 need approved issue and pull-request fixtures plus cleanup ownership; PUB-TC-009 needs a public tag, release, and downloadable asset in each repository; PUB-TC-011 and 012 need owner-provided collaborator and branch-protection evidence; PUB-TC-020 needs a maintenance window, explicit visibility-change authorization, and a named recovery owner. Results will validate canonical repository IDs, fields, links, index references, exact public heads, commands, environment, and logs. Any product defect will become a BUG-SUB; a missing controlled fixture will be reported as blocked, not failed.
