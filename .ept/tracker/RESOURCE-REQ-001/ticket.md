# RESOURCE-REQ-001: Provision Business Analyst (BA) agent for Foundry CLI project

## Description

﻿## Role Definition

**Role**: Business Analyst (BA)
**Project**: Foundry CLI Agentic Toolset — 21 Claude Code skills exposing 355 Palantir Foundry API v2 operations

## Context / Business Need

FEATURE-001 (Foundry CLI Requirements Analysis) is currently In Development with 23 DEV-STORYs created across 8 EPICs. All DEV-STORYs are in `New` status awaiting entry into the Analysis → Grooming → Development lifecycle.

A Business Analyst is required to:
- Execute BA-DES sub-tasks under FEATURE-001 to produce detailed business design specifications  
- Author and refine Given/When/Then acceptance criteria for all 23 DEV-STORYs  
- Bridge the gap between business requirements (SRS-001) and technical implementation  

No BA agent is currently provisioned. Proceeding without one will block the Design phase and leave acceptance criteria undefined for all DEV-STORYs.

## Responsibilities

1. Execute BA-DES sub-tasks under FEATURE-001: produce detailed business design specifications and user-facing documentation aligned with SRS-001
2. Author and refine acceptance criteria (Given/When/Then format) for all 23 DEV-STORYs, ensuring traceability to FEATURE-001 requirements
3. Collaborate with Solution Architect on integration between business requirements and technical architecture (SAD-001)
4. Review and validate DEV-STORY scope during Grooming: confirm scope, identify gaps, ensure acceptance criteria are testable
5. Support QA Engineer with user acceptance testing criteria and assist with BUG-SUB triage from a business perspective
6. Maintain requirements traceability matrix and business documentation throughout Phases 3 and 4
