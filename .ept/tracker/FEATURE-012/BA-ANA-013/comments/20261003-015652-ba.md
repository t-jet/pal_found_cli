## Business case and deliverable
Agents unfamiliar with Foundry need the distributed skills to explain platform concepts, operation effects, input meaning, and results so they can complete a supported task offline. Deliverable: one business analysis document for BA-ANA-013, with requirements, impact assessment, and testable acceptance criteria.

## Entry criteria
Given an agent with no prior Foundry knowledge and only installed skills and CLI, when it prepares a supported operation, then it can explain what the operation changes or reads, choose valid Foundry inputs, and interpret the result or failure without external guidance. Prior FEATURE-008 covers brief descriptions; FEATURE-009 covers JSON syntax and variants. Their business analyses and designs are related sources.
