## Business requirements (2026-10-03)
Source deliverable: `.ept/docs/deliverables/business_analysis/BA-ANA-013-offline-foundry-knowledge.md`, section 2.
- BR-012-01: Explain Foundry resources and relationships for novices.
- BR-012-02: Explain each capability purpose, workflow, prerequisites, and related resources.
- BR-012-03: Explain every supported operation effect and material side effect.
- BR-012-04: Explain positional, JSON, operation, and common option meanings, constraints, and behavioral differences.
- BR-012-05: Explain success results, identifiers, state, empty/partial results, paging, and pending work.
- BR-012-06: Explain failures and distinguish failure from pending or unknown final outcome.
- BR-012-07: Explain prerequisites and ordering for covered end-to-end tasks.
- BR-012-08: Give each operation a coherent input/effect/result example.
- BR-012-09: Support operation choice and interpretation offline using distributed skills and installed CLI.
- BR-012-10: Match supported CLI and authoritative Foundry behavior, with consistent concepts and change review.
- BR-012-11: Explain access, permission, request-volume, and data-volume limits without implying new access.
AC-012-01..13 in section 3 map these requirements to independently reviewable Given/When/Then cases.
